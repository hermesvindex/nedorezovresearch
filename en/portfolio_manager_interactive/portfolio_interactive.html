Portfolio constraints

Expected value, return, cash flow and stress scenarios are model estimates. Fees, taxes, spreads, instrument availability and individual constraints are excluded from the calculation. Before trading, verify the data date and the current terms of the selected instruments. Nedorezov Research does not receive, transmit, store or analyse questionnaire responses or the composition of your portfolio.

METHODOLOGY

How the portfolio is built

  1. A mandatory liquidity reserve is calculated.
  2. Risk capacity and risk tolerance are assessed separately.
  3. The final risk level is capped by the lower score.
  4. Instruments are filtered by horizon, access, liquidity and structure.
  5. Tri stsenariya prokhodyat stress-test and proverku kontsentratsiy.

Expected return is presented as a range and does not guarantee an outcome.
