METODIKA

Kak stroyatsya stsenarii

  1. A mandatory liquidity reserve is calculated.
  2. Risk capacity and risk tolerance are assessed separately.
  3. Totalvyy risk ogranichivaetsya bolee nizkoy otsenkoy.
  4. Instruments filtruyutsya by sroku, dostupu, likvidnosti and strukture.
  5. Tri stsenariya prokhodyat stress-test and proverku kontsentratsiy.
